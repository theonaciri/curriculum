2026Cargo security · MAORI MéxicoMexico

Ruta

Product lead & full-stack AI engineer (freelance)

An AI-native operations-center platform for a Mexican cargo-security company: live GPS fleet tracking, prioritized alerts (lost signal, suspected jammers, deviations, panic button), client-specific response protocols, escort coordination, carrier audits and incident timelines that assemble themselves for insurers. AI runs through the whole product: an assistant that works with the same data and permissions as the monitors — it watches night shifts through server-side rules, answers with cited sources, prepares shift handovers and reconstructs incidents — reachable from any AI client through a remote MCP server with OAuth, from the browser via WebMCP, by voice with OpenAI Realtime and from partner agents over A2A.

All 218 API operations open to AI agents — and not a single AI action without human approval.
